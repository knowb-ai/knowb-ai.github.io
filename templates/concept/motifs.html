Saber lines divide and underline. They never fill a surface, and appear at most once per component.

3 · World of Tomorrow shapes

Starburst, atom, boomerang, ringed planet. One per view, as illustration in its own solid panel, never behind text.

8×8 grid, crisp edges, two colors at most. Used in toasts, buttons and status chips; decorative unless they carry meaning.
